# Guteneo 1.2.8 — Gare departure boards and tickets

Gare gains split-flap departure boards, a station clock, a pausable announcement
display, and paper tickets with a dispatch timeline. The public and signed-in
halls use the same station design. The station is available in French, English,
German and Luxembourgish.

The departure board preserves additional pages during status refreshes and
keeps full destinations and exact approved prices available. Reduced-motion
preferences apply to the displays, and a manual announcement pause survives
pointer and focus changes. The ticket uses recorded provider acceptance for
its postmark date when that evidence exists.

This release changes the station client and its documentation. The existing
session, API, schema, approval, provider and sending rules apply to the station.
The browser suite includes synthetic fixtures for the station design and status
updates. Production verification is recorded separately after publication.
