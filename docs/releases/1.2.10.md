# Guteneo 1.2.10 — Worldwide fax and sending prices

Guteneo can quote an international fax number using the rate for its destination, including the applicable page, call and duration components. The customer approves a maximum before sending. A Cloudflare job refreshes the worldwide catalogue daily at 02:17 UTC. A failed or incomplete refresh leaves the previous catalogue available. Where there is no applicable reference, the commercial rate is EUR0.25 per page excluding tax.

Email uses the applicable transport reference or EUR1.56 per thousand recipients excluding tax. Postal mail uses the Pingen calculator for the analyzed letter or a saved price for the same configuration. The first-use postal rate during an outage is EUR2.50 per letter excluding tax. Additional services appear in the total presented for approval.

New references apply to subsequent quotes. Guteneo absorbs supplier differences above the agreed amount. Country restrictions imposed by a carrier, address availability, account funding and recipient consent still apply. Production schema and publication can be verified independently of actual delivery.
