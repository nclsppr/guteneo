# Guteneo 1.3.1 — Worldwide fax and continuous sending prices

Guteneo 1.3.1 prepares international fax quotes and keeps fax, email and postal quotes available when a supplier price cannot be refreshed. Customers see the estimate or price and approve the spending limit before sending. Compatible assistants can discover nine workflow skills over MCP, including the updated fax, email and postal instructions.

## Sending prices

Guteneo prepares a quote for an international fax number using the applicable destination, page, call and duration rates. A Cloudflare job refreshes the catalog daily at 02:17 UTC. A failed or incomplete refresh leaves the previous catalog available. When no applicable price is available, Guteneo estimates EUR 0.25 per page excluding tax. The customer sees the estimate and approves a firm ceiling. Guteneo absorbs any supplier expense above that ceiling.

Email uses its applicable transport reference or the published rate of EUR 1.56 per thousand recipients excluding tax. Postal mail uses the Pingen calculator for the analyzed letter or a saved price for the same configuration. During a price outage the first-use postal tariff is EUR 2.50 per letter excluding tax. Optional services appear in the total before approval.

New prices apply to subsequent quotes. Provider destination rules, valid addresses, account funding and the required approval still apply. Production schema and served application receipts establish publication. Actual delivery requires its own evidence.

## Connected assistant workflows

This release carries forward the nine remote MCP workflow skills introduced in [Guteneo 1.3.0](https://github.com/nclsppr/guteneo/blob/aba15b9d790a10c1f23430bb5177c70028d0fc0e/docs/releases/1.3.0.md). Connected hosts can discover the workflow instructions, inspect their manifests and verify the files they read. The remote catalog and downloadable plugins share the updated fax and postal instructions. Tool annotations describe calls to external services. Each tool still requires its own authorization.
