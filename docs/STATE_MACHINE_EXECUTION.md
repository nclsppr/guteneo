## Completion criteria

- Every audited app-owned lifecycle has an explicit implementation contract or a documented decision to retain its already explicit/simple mechanism. Do not add abstractions merely to increase a refactor count.
- High-risk dispatch, secure sharing, upload, postal, generation/distribution and client recovery decisions use integrated typed models where these eliminate dispersed or contradictory rules.
- Campaign completion, safe dead-letter resolution and account deletion have executable, honest end-to-end local workflows with adversarial evidence; no fake completion or blind replay.
- Tenant/current authority, immutable approval, atomic quota/credit/outbox and unknown provider no-resend invariants remain enforced at persistence boundaries.
- Source, feature/role/journey registry, authored engineering explanations, migration order and qualification limits agree.
- Final stable-source type/lint/unit/security/browser/build/documentation checks pass at their appropriate scope. Production/provider/device proof remains separate.

## Product decisions

- Campaign completion means operational and financial closure of immutable nonempty membership, including postal handover as the postal execution boundary. It never asserts universal receipt or releases a financial hold.
- A dead-letter diagnostic can resolve only after the original command is durably closed. Resolution changes diagnostic status and writes one audit receipt; it never requeues or sends.
- Account deletion must preserve shared workspaces, necessary immutable evidence and unresolved liabilities, block every authentication surface/recreation while processing, verify required storage/identity steps and remain processing through uncertain external effects. A disabled profile cannot be relabeled completed deletion.

## Implementation outcome

- Small integrated decision modules replace dispersed high-risk transition
  choices without a second persisted state store or statechart dependency.
- Final source and authority predicates close reproduced upload-purge and
  dataset/mapping revocation races. Browser continuations reject obsolete
  identity/view generations, including leave-and-return and partial adoption.
- Migration 0075 closes eligible frozen campaigns; 0076 adds audited no-replay
  incident resolution; 0077 adds the separate reviewed deletion lifecycle.
- Administrator control/resolution batches use one authority instant and paired
  effects, including expiry inside a transaction. Deletion verifies exact
  targets, fences leases and snapshots, drains storage debt, and preserves a
  live workspace administrator throughout processing.
- Existing provider maintenance, single-use capabilities, process locks and
  simple controllers are retained where they already express the contract.
- Developer contracts, the authored engineering guide, role/journey registry,
  MCP instructions and deterministic plugin archives are synchronized. The
  local MCP catalogue remains 83 tools; no assistant authority is added.
