Guteneo's public films are editable Remotion compositions backed by local media,
not screen recordings of an automatically available delivery service. Replaying
an approved film uses stored narration and makes no speech-provider call. Creating
new speech, approving a new film and publishing it are separate operations.

The active catalog has twelve films: horizontal introduction, vertical
introduction and role overview in each of `fr`, `en`, `de`, `lb`. Its authority is
`packages/contracts/src/public-videos.json`, with actual durations and matching
posters/captions. The server's `apps/api/src/public-video-manifest.json` records
served MP4 byte counts and hashes. Neither a historical filename nor a generated
provider result selects a film for the website.

## Install and inspect locally

From the repository root, install the locked application and film dependencies.
The film package pins Remotion **4.0.526**. Keep all Remotion packages at their
locked, matching versions; an upgrade needs its own qualification.

```sh
npm ci
npm --prefix videos/guteneo-film ci
npm --prefix videos/guteneo-film run lint
npm --prefix videos/guteneo-film run dev
```

Open the exact local address printed by Remotion Studio. Select a composition in
`videos/guteneo-film/src/Root.tsx`. The `Natural-C` folder contains dynamic
narration-timed introductions and the English/German/Luxembourgish role versions;
French roles retain their approved natural timeline. Frame-driven React animation
lives in `src/scenes/`, `src/landscape/`, `src/vertical/` and `src/roles/`.

FFmpeg, ffprobe and cwebp must also be available for local regeneration. Fonts,
images and approved audio are local. The renderer has no customer account,
business binding or communication secret. An ordinary Studio preview is a local
review, not a publication or provider qualification.

## Change a story without changing the product's promises

Start from complete sentences and the actual workflow. The four roles and
independent supervisor permissions come from `packages/contracts/src/roles.ts`.
Preparation, human review, approval, provider acceptance and delivery must retain
their distinct meanings. A film does not grant a mandate, make a channel available
or certify a marketplace integration.

Use only synthetic product captures. Keep their demonstration disclosures and
provenance; do not retouch the DOM to invent a feature. Reconstructed assistant
screens are labelled advertising reconstructions, not real chat or published
widget evidence. Printed-paper, envelope and fax scenes illustrate uses; they
are not delivery receipts.

Review factual claims and the complete script before synthesis. Technical
instructions and code stay English; narration, captions and on-screen wording
may use their declared language. Localize all three media companions together:
film, poster and captions. The player resolves UI/account language and must never
silently substitute another language's voice.

## Produce new narration only when authorized

Existing approved speech should be reused. For a new authorized narration batch,
follow the actual **method C: three contextual blocks** rather than synthesizing
isolated visual headlines. Introductions group eleven paragraphs **4/4/3**;
role films group six paragraphs **2/2/2**. Horizontal and iPhone introductions
share one narration per language.

1. Run the complete script through native ElevenLabs **Enhance** and accept
   **Keep**. Preserve the exact native result in `enhancement.json`. The connected
   Creative Studio path did not supply native Enhance; the recorded workflow used
   authorized Safari account UI.
2. Review the wording and permissions. If needed, record calm-tone edits in
   `editorialEdits`; `[thoughtful]` and `[short pause]` describe the retained
   direction. Keep the native enhancement separate from the final edited script.
3. Inspect the live model schema, choose the actual Manon voice and set
   `model_parameters.language_code` explicitly to `fr`, `en`, `de` or `lb`.
   Use `eleven_v4`, not an assumed similarly named model. The display name is a
   selection aid; the approved library retains the actual provider identity.
4. Generate two variations per block through the authorized Creative Studio
   connection and retain the second by its real session/generation identity.
   Returned-media order is not selection evidence. If an outcome is uncertain,
   inspect that existing flow/session instead of generating again.
5. Archive original selected MP3s, exact scripts, filtered receipts, durations,
   byte counts and hashes. Keep credentials, signed URLs and private project
   identifiers out of public documentation. Estimates do not prove billed usage.

This is an account-bound, potentially paid phase; the offline render commands
below do not perform it. The old local `narration:generate` adapter remains blocked
before network with `API_ADAPTER_UNQUALIFIED`. Plugin access does not qualify that
adapter, and `narration/voices.example.json` is a historical configuration example,
not the authority for the currently published voice.

## Fit scenes to the voice, never accelerate the voice

Decode complete MP3s to mono PCM24 at 44.1 kHz. Establish boundaries from word
timestamps and acoustic pauses without prompting a recognizer with the expected
script. Preserve real transcription differences. In this batch, French, English
and German used Whisper word timing; Luxembourgish used native Scribe JSON after
local recognition proved inadequate. Recognition does not certify pronunciation
or create missing words/timestamps.

Retain every source sample in order at **`tempoFactor:1`**. Lengthen scenes to
actual speech, with readable minimum holds and interblock pauses. Do not trim a
last word, time-stretch a voice or force a localized narration into the old
56-second instrumental timeline.

All films retain the approved **five-second silent logo**, exactly 150 frames at
30 fps; introductions also retain **two silent Signature seconds**. Music remains
at a constant **0.22 gain** during speech, pauses and the logo, with the original
score's cadence/end fade. Voice normalization and peak checks are separate. The
old George mix's between-sentence music ducking is historical, not this policy.

Each of the eight immutable directories
`narration/releases/{introduction,roles}-{fr,en,de,lb}-natural-c-v1/` retains
scripts, enhancement, three MP3 sources, canonical WAV cuts, timing/alignment,
receipts and hashes. Together they hold 24 selected MP3s and 68 PCM24 cuts. The
builder rejects overwriting an existing snapshot.

MP3, canonical PCM and cut hashes remain strict. Decoder qualification permits
only a documented difference of at most eight PCM24 least-significant bits with
an unchanged sample count. Scalar FFmpeg decoding uses `-cpuflags 0` to avoid
CPU-specific optimized paths; this bounded decoder allowance does not permit
changing canonical WAVs or published films.

## Rebuild approved films offline

Run these commands from the repository root:

```sh
npm run test:videos:narration
npm run videos:render
```

The renderer qualifies narration libraries before public writes, renders musical
bases, remixes stored speech while preserving base H.264 packets, then writes
posters, VTT captions and the server manifest. It requires no ElevenLabs call.
It writes generated public media: review the resulting diff and hashes before
including them in a release.

For a bounded subset, pass the existing renderer's explicit selectors:

```sh
npm run videos:render -- --locales de,lb --kind introduction
npm run videos:render -- --locales en --kind roles --skip-existing
```

`--skip-existing` still checks the manifest hash and matching poster/captions.
It is not permission to reuse an arbitrary file. Normal output masters and
`render-proof.json` are under `videos/guteneo-film/out/localized/`; public movies
and posters are under `apps/web/public/videos/`.

A new library has a separate initialization path.
`scripts/build-natural-library.mjs --input <prepared-directory>` validates and
freezes a prepared library; it does not synthesize audio.
`scripts/render-natural-sources.mjs --libraries <snapshot-id>` writes musical
sources only under ignored `out/`. These are controlled authoring steps, not
shortcuts for replacing an approved snapshot or updating the catalog implicitly.

## Preserve the approved visual ending

The visual base is the approved V5 composition even when natural narration
extends the film. Landscape and iPhone scenes are individually recomposed, not
cropped versions of each other. Preserve the simple unpostmarked opening mark and
the large final **official V5 halftone stamp, postmark and `guteneo.com`**. Do not
regenerate or redraw that ending to match a new illustration style.

Use paper **`#f6f5ef`**, ink **`#181b22`**, cobalt **`#2450db`**, local EB Garamond
and IBM Plex Sans/Mono. Keep `guteneo` lettering lowercase and ink black under the
existing video composition rules. The stamp already contains the signature; add
no duplicate domain. The engineering header's separate dark-mode exception is
not a video-brand change.

Image/font/audio provenance lives beside the assets: `public/images/PROVENANCE.md`,
`public/frontend/provenance.md`, `public/fonts/` licenses and `public/audio/README.md`
in the film project. The original soundtrack is procedural local synthesis, not
a third-party music extract. Preserve licenses and generated-asset disclosures;
a provider receipt does not by itself establish rights for an unrelated asset.

## Verify, then publish through the release process

```sh
node --test tests/security/public-video-assets.test.mjs
ffprobe -v error -show_streams -show_format -of json \
  apps/web/public/videos/guteneo-horizontal-v8-en.mp4
ffmpeg -v error -i apps/web/public/videos/guteneo-horizontal-v8-en.mp4 \
  -f null -
```

Check all changed outputs, not just this example: H.264/AAC stereo, 30 fps,
fast-start, intended dimensions, complete decoding, bounded file size below
25 MiB, exact catalog duration, synchronized captions, suitable posters and the
unchanged final logo hold. Inspect desktop and mobile framing, active-language
media, keyboard controls, sound and reduced-motion behavior.

Listen to every changed language continuously. Automated ASR, peak measurements
or successful decoding do not prove a natural accent, complete words or human
approval. Only the French role film carries the recorded user-listening approval;
other libraries retain pending critical-listening evidence. A phone viewport is
not a physical-device test.

Local rendering does not publish files. Follow [changes and releases](/engineering/releases/)
for the exact merged commit, CI, clean main checkout and authorized deployment.
Then verify the actual public assets, hashes, byte-range behavior, captions and
player on the served release. No film build sends a fax, email or postal letter.

## Keep the Gutenberg production separate

The cinematic Gutenberg archive preserves the approved proposal-2 character,
François-Louis narration and Manon signature. Do not replace its voice with the
public-film default or claim that its revised seven environments have already
been rendered. Approved voice files need no resynthesis or speed correction.
Its current handoff distinguishes existing short previews from unfinished full
exports.

The archive can be recovered and checked locally without a provider account:

```sh
python3 videos/guteneo-gutenberg/tools/restore_large_media.py
python3 videos/guteneo-gutenberg/tools/verify_archive.py
```

Restoration reconstructs exact bytes from tracked chunks without transcoding.
Historical localized prompts and voices stay immutable. Read its
`TECHNICAL_HANDOFF.md`, `STORYBOARD_AND_PROMPTS.md` and `DECISIONS_AND_STATUS.md`
before resuming work; source availability, approved speech, animation quality,
finished export and publication remain separate evidence.
